Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Explore Hawke's Bay handles personal information, following the information privacy principles in the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

Only the details you type into our forms: your name, email address, optional phone number and your message or place suggestion.

Why we collect it

To reply to your question or review your place suggestion. We do not use it for anything else.

Emails and consent

We only email you if you tick the consent box, as required by the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007. Every email will include a working unsubscribe link.

Storage and sharing

Your details are not sold or shared with anyone. Comments and ratings you add on attraction pages are not saved anywhere. Because this is a student project, forms are not sent to a real server.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you by using the Contact & Feedback page.
