Our Story

Explore Hawke's Bay started with a simple problem: the big tourism websites list hundreds of businesses, and visitors told us they just wanted to know where to go first.

So we built a small, curated guide. Every place on this site has been chosen because it shows something special about the region – its wine, its coastline, or its Art Deco history.

Hawke's Bay, or Te Matau-a-Māui, stretches along the east coast of the North Island. The region's main towns are Napier, Hastings and Havelock North, with smaller communities such as Wairoa in the north and Central Hawke's Bay in the south.

The region is known for its warm, dry climate, which is perfect for growing grapes and fruit. In 1931 a major earthquake changed the landscape and destroyed much of Napier, and the city was rebuilt in the Art Deco style that visitors come to see today.

Our aim is to keep this site short, honest and easy to use on a phone, so you can spend less time planning and more time exploring.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Explore Hawke's Bay handles personal information, following the information privacy principles in the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

Only the details you type into our forms: your name, email address, optional phone number and your message or place suggestion.

Why we collect it

To reply to your question or review your place suggestion. We do not use it for anything else.

Emails and consent

We only email you if you tick the consent box, as required by the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007. Every email will include a working unsubscribe link.

Storage and sharing

Your details are not sold or shared with anyone. Comments and ratings you add on attraction pages are not saved anywhere. Because this is a student project, forms are not sent to a real server.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you by using the Contact & Feedback page.
